About

A quieter kind of dentistry.

Most dental work is judged by what nobody notices. A crown that catches the light the way the tooth beside it does. A filling that stops being a filling. The best outcome is usually the one that goes unremarked.

That standard shapes how the clinic runs: longer appointments, treatment planned before an instrument is picked up, and a preference for keeping natural structure wherever it can be kept.

    How we think about a tooth

    A tooth is never
    treated alone.

    Colour, angle and edge are set by the teeth on either side of the one being worked on. Get a single unit right in isolation and it still reads as a repair.

    1. One toothEvery plan starts with a single unit — its structure, its wear, what is worth keeping.
    2. In contextMatched against its neighbours in daylight, not against a shade chart.
    3. One lineTen teeth carry a single line across the face. The eye finds a break in it long before it finds the tooth.
